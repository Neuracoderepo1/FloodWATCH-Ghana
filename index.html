How to read this. Statuses come from a conservative classifier: a verified official alert or confirmed incident always takes precedence, and without fresh data, adequate spatial coverage, validation against multiple flood events, validated exposure and hydrology data, and event-validated thresholds, the status is “Risk uncertain — data insufficient”. Those validation steps are not yet complete, so no High, Watch or “No elevated signal” rating is shown. Elevation comes from a roughly 90 m grid, and waterways from OpenStreetMap, which can be incomplete in parts of Accra, so absence of a mapped drain does not mean absence of one. This is not a validated flood model. Past hours are model estimates, not rain-gauge readings. For official alerts follow NADMO and GMet.
