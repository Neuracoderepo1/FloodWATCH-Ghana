# Public wording (draft) for the FloodWatch Ghana page

Status: draft for the owner's review. Plain English first; a translation into Twi, Ga and
other languages should be done and checked by native speakers, not by machine.
Every claim below is backed by `docs/evidence-log.md`. Do not add phone numbers or
agency names here that have not been checked against the agency's own site.

## Banner (always visible)
**This page is not a flood forecast and gives no official warning.**
We show rainfall and terrain information for context only. For official alerts, follow
GMet (Ghana Meteorological Agency) and NADMO. If water is rising near you, move to higher
ground and follow local authorities, whatever this page says.

## What each status means
- **Emergency alert — official source:** a verified alert from an official agency is in force. Follow the agency's instructions.
- **Confirmed incident — urgent action:** flooding has been confirmed in this area.
- **Unverified report — verify promptly:** someone has reported flooding here; it has not been checked.
- **High / Watch:** *(not shown yet)* will appear only after the page has been tested against several real floods.
- **No elevated signal detected:** *(not shown yet)* will never mean "safe". It would mean only that our checks found nothing unusual.
- **Risk uncertain — data insufficient:** *shown for every area today.* We do not yet have enough tested data to rate this area.

## Why you see "Risk uncertain"
We tested our approach against the 28–29 June 2026 flood. Rainfall models can badly underestimate
heavy local storms: GMet reported 169.2 mm for the day, while the model data we use showed only a
few millimetres at a point where streets were under water. Until we have measured rainfall from
more stations, and have checked the page against several floods, we would rather say "uncertain"
than give a rating that could be wrong.

## What the numbers on the page are
- **Rainfall numbers** come from a weather model, not from rain gauges, and may be much lower than what actually fell.
- **Elevation and "near a waterway"** come from a roughly 90 m height map and OpenStreetMap, which can miss small drains. A missing drain on the map does not mean there isn't one.
- A "low-lying" tag describes the ground, not today's weather.

## Short version for social media / WhatsApp (under 280 characters)
FloodWatch Ghana shows rainfall and terrain info for Greater Accra. It is NOT a flood forecast and gives no official warnings. We rate nothing as "safe". For alerts, follow GMet and NADMO. Help us test it: tell us what you saw on 29 June 2026.

## Do not say
- "Safe", "low risk", "no flood expected", or anything that tells people they can relax.
- That the page "predicted" or "would have predicted" any flood.
- Any rating, count or percentage that has not passed the scorecard on several events.
