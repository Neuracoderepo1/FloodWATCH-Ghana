**Subject:** Request for historical rainfall data, Accra stations, 2018 to date (including 28–29 June 2026)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing on behalf of NeuraCode Studios to request historical rainfall observations for the Accra area, for an independent, non-commercial back-test of rainfall thresholds behind a public flood-awareness web page for Greater Accra. The page will not issue official warnings and will direct users to GMet and NADMO for official alerts. We want to test it against measured rainfall before presenting any risk rating to the public.

**Data requested**
1. Daily rainfall totals, and hourly values where they exist, for Accra-area stations: Kotoka International Airport, Legon, Tema, Weija, Achimota, Kasoa, and any other station GMet considers part of the Accra network.
2. Period: 1 January 2018 to the latest available date, and in particular 28 and 29 June 2026.
3. For each station: name, coordinates, elevation, and any changes in location or instrument during the period.
4. For each daily value: the accumulation window and time zone (for example 06:00 to 06:00 UTC or 00:00 to 24:00 UTC), and flags for missing, estimated or quality-controlled values.
5. For the 169.2 mm daily figure reported for 29 June 2026: the station it refers to and the observation window, if this can be shared.

**Format:** CSV or Excel is ideal; any structured format is fine.

**Use and credit:** The data will be used only to evaluate rainfall thresholds and will be cited as GMet data in any published results. We will not redistribute raw station data without your permission.

**Questions:** Please let us know what forms, fees or licence terms apply, who the right contact is if this should go to another office, and how you would like GMet credited.

Thank you for your time and for the work GMet does for the public.
